name: answer-questions
description: Answer questions about work, projects and people from the tracker.
---

Use for "what's on my plate", "what's overdue in WEB", "who owns X", "status of the launch".

- Use the tools; never answer from memory or from the Slack thread alone when the tracker knows.
- "My", "me", "I" mean the person asking (`list_tasks` with assignee "me").
- Project status: `summarise_project`, then lead with what needs attention (overdue, unassigned,
  blocked), then counts. As a card: the counts as Fields, a pie of tasks by status, and a table of
  what needs attention.
- "What's on my plate": a card with a table of their open tasks (key, title, status, due), soonest
  due first, overdue ones marked.
- Keep lists to the ten most relevant items and say how many more there are.
- If the tracker has nothing, say so plainly. If the person may lack access, say you can only see
  what they can.
